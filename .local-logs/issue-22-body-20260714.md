## What Past Writes Mean

The July 12 read-only audit partitioned 29,449 retained staged source versions:

- 25,448 exactly represented now.
- 1,435 inventory-only records that never claimed a write.
- 2,548 excluded by current format rules.
- 18 intact conversation-export source versions with no exact current or selected-snapshot representation.

The 18 project to 22,220 current-rule rows from 816,920,494 source bytes. All exceeded the former 10 MiB scan cap, so a historical silent skip is plausible. The old per-run runtime version was not retained, so that is not proof. A future replay may publish a new, honestly dated `historical-recovery` receipt; it must not fabricate the missing original write-time receipt.
